# Synopsis (for translators)

Built from the script itself (prologue, intro, endings, a term pass over the events); no web guide exists. Gaps are
marked; translators who learn more report it (`UNSURE:`), and the controller updates this file between waves.

## Setting
- **Prologue (creation myth):** the Supreme God (至高神) wants to set the great Law and take a wife from the seven
  goddesses he made. The Goddess of Darkness offers herself (freedom to walk as one wills, quiet rest in the dark);
  the Goddess of Light offers herself too (the light of truth, warmth of the soul). He can't wed both, so he rules:
  "Fight, and decide which is fit for the true Law." So begins the Holy-Demon War (聖魔戦争).
- **The war:** 11 Holy Beast Kings (聖獣王, Light side) against 11 Demon Beast Kings (魔獣王, Dark side). Each
  is a human leader with an army and tamed beasts (ビースト; a Beast Master commands them). Fighting between kings
  is forbidden until the First Banner (第一の旗) sounds, like a bell, in the sky once all 22 kings have come down.
  Cities across the world fall to Light or Dark (祝福 "blessing" = a city won for a side).
- **The hero:** one of the Holy Beast Kings (name entered by the player, `{var:02}`), advised by Rundstedt
  (ルントシュテット, formal, calls the hero 王 "my king"), reporting to Lang (ラング), High Priest of Light, at
  Meisilva Castle. Other Holy Beast Kings are allies who join or report in (the 12 "generals": Lorenz, Napier,
  Eibesfeldt, Brian, Charcot, Zona, Bork, Ekers, Wilson "10th Holy Beast King", Pal, Prana, Rabius); they can be
  recruited into the party (CE recruit lines).
- **Rival:** Gaius (ガイアス), on the Dark side, who knew the hero before (an ending: "I didn't want to meet you
  like this"). Barnaios (バルナイオス) serves the Dark and holds a castle late in the game.
- **Side plots seen:** the kidnapped Princess Arianna; the Blue Whale (シロナガスクジラ), a giant monster wrecking
  coastal towns (Rook, Mellow); Death (死神) freed by Zona after Hannes's soul is lost (heretic mage, 反魂の外法);
  a foreign merchant selling a ship; a baseball gag (Nomo Shuary); a Snow Festival; kappa returning to Boyle.

## Endings (5; late reveals - don't foreshadow)
- The war is a **game of the gods**: the goddesses bicker over "next time", the Supreme God rules humans by the
  winning side's Law, and the Legendary Beast (伝説の獣) burns the losing side's cities.
- Light wins, hero obeys: the Goddess of Light, in near-mad glee, orders the Legendary Beast to destroy the Dark;
  the hero sees the fires and doubts. Light wins by default / the hero rebels: "humans should live by human law".
  Dark wins: Gaius spares the hero, or Barnaios exults and the Beast attacks. The Goddess of Light is presented as
  benevolent until the endings: keep her early lines warm and sincere.
- Epilogue: the true Holy-Demon War is the one in people's hearts.
